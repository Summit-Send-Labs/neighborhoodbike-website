Finding the Shop

GPS Will Get You Close.
But, it can still be confusing.

GPS will get you to the right road — but a few houses along the way are also Number 10, and the numbers are hard to read. Look for forest green siding with purple trim and a sign with our logo and "10 Ontario Green" out front. Free parking is on the right, in front of the wooden "Fair Fountain Green" sign — once you're parked, the shop is above the garage right behind you.

Turn-by-Turn

Photos From The Road.

What you'll actually see approaching the shop. If anything looks different than the last photo, you've probably gone too far — turn around.

Step 1
Turn off of Wellington Road onto Bridge Street. (Heading west on Wellington — Breckenridge ski area is straight ahead.)
Step 2
Stay left to keep on Bridge Street as you enter the neighborhood.
Step 3
The shop is on the left side of the road — this is roughly the halfway point through the neighborhood.
Step 4
As you approach, you'll see the shop in green with purple trim on the left. Parking is on the right.
Step 5
Look for the Neighborhood Bike sign between the two garage doors.
Step 6
Park in front of the wood structure with "Fair Fountain Green" signage. The shop is right behind you, above the garage.
